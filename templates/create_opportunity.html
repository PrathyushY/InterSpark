Tips for Creating Great Opportunities

Be Specific and Clear

  • Use descriptive titles that clearly indicate the role
  • Specify the time commitment and duration
  • Be clear about remote vs. in-person work

Highlight Learning

  • Emphasize skills students will develop
  • Mention mentorship and guidance available
  • Include any certificates or recognition offered

Set Realistic Expectations

  • Match requirements to the experience level
  • Be realistic about time commitments
  • Clearly state if it's paid or unpaid

Make It Appealing

  • Use engaging language and positive tone
  • Include your organization's mission
  • Add an attractive image if possible
